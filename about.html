About Nirvan

A fest built by builders, for builders.

NIRVAN '26 brings together developers, designers, security researchers and technology enthusiasts for two days of hands-on challenges — not lectures. It's GEHU Haldwani's flagship technical fest, back with a sharper focus on things you can actually build, break and ship.

Across five arenas — hackathon, treasure hunt, e-sports, capture-the-flag and workshops — you'll compete in real time, meet people who build things you use, and leave with something to show for it.

  • 12–13 Oct 2026
  • GEHU Campus, Haldwani
  • 2 days
  • 5 events
What's on
  • Hackathon

    Build, code and ship a working solution against the clock. Team of 2–4, ₹15,000 prize pool.

  • Treasure Hunt

    Clues, logic and teamwork across campus. Race other teams to the final marker.

  • E-Sports

    Competitive gaming arena — strategy, reflexes and squads battling for the leaderboard.

  • CTF

    Cryptography, web security, forensics and reverse engineering. Find the flags, prove your skills.

  • Workshop

    Hands-on sessions with practitioners — learn something you'll actually use, free entry.
